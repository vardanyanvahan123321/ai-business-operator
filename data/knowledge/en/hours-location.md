# Opening hours and location

## Opening hours
We are open Monday to Saturday from 09:00 to 19:00. Sunday is a day off. On public holidays the workshop is closed.

## Address
The workshop is at 1 Demo Street, Yerevan. There is free customer parking in front of the entrance.

## Contacts
You can reach us by phone at +374 10 000000 or by email at service@demo-auto.example. Messages in Telegram and through the website form are answered around the clock by the assistant.

## Waiting area
While your car is being serviced you can wait in the lounge with free Wi-Fi and coffee.
