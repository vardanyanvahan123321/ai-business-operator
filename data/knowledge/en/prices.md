# Services and prices

## Oil change
An oil change costs 12,000 AMD. The price includes the work and a new oil filter check; the oil itself is paid separately or you can bring your own. The job takes about 40 minutes.

## Computer diagnostics
Computer diagnostics costs 8,000 AMD and takes about 30 minutes. If you then repair the car with us, the diagnostics fee is deducted from the repair bill.

## Brake pads replacement
Replacing brake pads costs 15,000 AMD per axle (work only, pads are paid separately). It takes about one hour.

## Tire fitting
Tire fitting costs 10,000 AMD for a set of four wheels, balancing included. It takes about 45 minutes.

## Wheel alignment
Wheel alignment costs 14,000 AMD and takes about one hour.

## Air conditioner recharge
An A/C recharge costs 18,000 AMD, including a leak test. It takes about 50 minutes.

## Engine repair
Engine repair starts from 60,000 AMD. The exact price is given only after diagnostics, because it depends on the fault and the parts needed.
