# Warranty and frequent questions

## Warranty
We give a 6-month warranty on all work performed. Parts bought from us carry the manufacturer's warranty. The warranty does not cover parts supplied by the customer.

## Car brands
We service all passenger car brands, including Toyota, Mercedes-Benz, BMW, Hyundai, Kia, Nissan and Lada. We do not service trucks or motorcycles.

## Towing
We do not have our own tow truck, but we can recommend a partner towing service in Yerevan.

## How long repairs take
Routine maintenance is done the same day. Complex engine repairs take from two to five working days, depending on parts delivery.
