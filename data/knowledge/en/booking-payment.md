# Booking and payment

## How to book
To book a visit, send us the service you need, a convenient date and time, and a contact phone number. We confirm every booking with a phone call. Booking is free.

## Cancelling or moving a booking
You can cancel or reschedule a booking free of charge at least two hours before the appointment. Just write to us or call.

## Payment methods
We accept cash, bank cards (Visa, Mastercard, ArCa) and bank transfers for companies. Payment is made after the work is finished.

## Invoices
An invoice is sent automatically when a booking is registered. Companies can request an invoice with their legal details.

## Spare parts
You may bring your own spare parts and oil. We also order original and quality aftermarket parts; delivery usually takes one to three days.
